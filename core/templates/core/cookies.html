Cookie Policy

Updated 9 September 2026

Essential cookies support sign-in, request security and your chosen privacy settings. Optional browsing history and usage analytics are off unless you opt in.

Optional account-linked activity

If you allow usage analytics, administrators can see submitted search terms, pages and products viewed, the time, and your account when signed in. Anonymous views cannot be matched to an account. Password, checkout, payment, loan and administration pages are excluded from general page tracking. Withdraw this choice through Cookie settings.

What is stored

  • sessionid: identifies a sign-in or cart session. It is used for account functions, not optional advertising.
  • csrftoken: protects submitted forms from forged requests.
  • czConsent: stores your choices and their date for up to 180 days.
  • czRecentlyViewed (local storage): remembers up to 12 product listings only when you allow recently viewed products. It remains until cleared, you withdraw permission, or browser storage is cleared.
  • czScrollResults (session storage): helps return you to search results during navigation and is removed after use.

Your choices

Choose “Reject optional” to use essential functions only, “Accept optional” to enable both optional categories, or select categories and save. You can change your mind at any time. Turning off browsing history removes that history from this browser. Choices apply to this browser, not automatically to your other devices.

Analytics and external services

With analytics enabled, the site records submitted search terms, result counts, and product interactions to understand use of the marketplace. Essential security and access logs are separate. No advertising pixel is enabled by this policy. Links to WhatsApp or other sites open those services only when you follow them; their own policies then apply. Product and advertiser images may be delivered from external image hosts.

See our Privacy Policy for account data and external AI processing.
