Order Policy

How our China-to-Zambia ordering system works — deposits, refunds, delivery times, and tracking.

📋 Please read and understand how ordering works before placing an order.
1Order Confirmation

After placing an order on the website, ChinaZed staff reviews the order details before processing to ensure accuracy and availability.

2Deposit Requirement

A 35% deposit is required to process any order. This confirms commitment and allows us to secure the item from China.

The remaining 65% balance is paid after the goods arrive in Zambia.

3Refund Policy
  • Deposit is refundable if the item is not delivered within the agreed time.
  • Refund applies if the supplier fails to fulfill the order.
  • Refund applies if the product becomes unavailable before purchase.
4Delivery Timeline

Each product has an estimated delivery range displayed on the website. Delivery times may vary due to:

  • Shipping delays
  • Customs clearance
  • Supplier processing time
  • Logistics and transport conditions
5Order Tracking

Log in to your profile to track order progress through these stages:

Confirmed Purchased Shipped In Transit Arrived in Zambia Ready for Collection Successful Delayed
6Receipts

You can generate a receipt once the goods have arrived in Zambia, or when the order is ready for collection or delivery.
