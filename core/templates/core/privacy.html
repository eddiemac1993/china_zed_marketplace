Privacy Policy

Updated 9 September 2026

SWIFTFIND VENTURES LIMITED (Local Company, registration ID 120261053220) operates ChinaZed in Lusaka, Zambia. Contact swiftfindzm@gmail.com about personal data. We do not currently have a walk-in office.

Optional account-linked activity

If you allow usage analytics, administrators can see pages and products viewed, the time, and your account when signed in. Anonymous views cannot be matched to an account. Password, checkout, payment, loan and administration pages are excluded from general page tracking. Withdraw this choice through Cookie settings.

Information and purpose

  • Accounts: email and a password stored as a password hash; optional profile details help identify and contact you. A profile photograph is optional.
  • Orders: chosen products, amounts, recipient phone, collection centre or delivery address, order notes, payment references/proof and status updates are used to fulfil and support orders. Provide a delivery address only for direct delivery.
  • Submissions: product requests, advertiser images, supplier details and messages are used to respond to the service you request. Uploaded ads and approved product listings are public.
  • Loan services: loan requests and records can include amounts, repayment details and contact information; staff forms also support identity, employment and next-of-kin information. Do not provide this information for ordinary shopping. Ask support about the lender and required disclosures before applying.
  • Technical information: session/security information and hosting access logs support site operation. Optional browsing history and analytics are controlled in Cookie settings.

Necessary processing and optional choices

We use account/order information to provide the service you request and meet applicable recordkeeping obligations. Optional analytics, browsing history and external AI use have separate choices; refusing them does not prevent ordinary shopping. Reading this notice is not consent to unrelated marketing. We do not ask for your payment PIN, online banking password or full card details.

External providers and sharing

The site is hosted on PythonAnywhere. Email and delivery/payment providers may process the details needed for the relevant transaction. Product images may load from supplier image hosts, which receive technical request information. Following a WhatsApp link opens WhatsApp. These providers may operate outside Zambia.

AI questions and photo analysis

Basic help is available without external AI. If you select external AI, your submitted question is sent to OpenAI. Recent order totals are not automatically attached. Do not put passwords, identity documents or payment credentials in questions. Supplier/admin photo-analysis tools send the selected product photo to OpenAI when used; avoid photos containing personal information. External-provider terms and processing arrangements apply.

Keeping and deleting data

Account, order and upload records remain stored for account services, fulfilment, disputes and any applicable legal obligations. There is not yet a published fixed deletion schedule for every record category. Contact us to request a retention explanation, correction or deletion. We will explain any information that must be retained. Browser history is removable through cookie settings.

Your rights

You may request access to your data, correction, deletion where applicable, information about processing and recipients, and withdrawal of optional consent. We may need proportionate information to verify a request. Withdrawal does not undo processing already lawfully performed. You may raise a concern with Zambia's Data Protection Commissioner.

Security and policy changes

Keep your password private and tell us about suspected unauthorised access. No service can promise absolute security. Material changes will be described in an updated notice; continued browsing does not authorise new optional processing.
