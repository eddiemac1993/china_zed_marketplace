Refund Policy

Updated 9 September 2026

This policy applies to ChinaZed, operated by SWIFTFIND VENTURES LIMITED. It does not remove any rights you have under Zambian consumer law.

Request a cancellation, return or refund

Email swiftfindzm@gmail.com with your order number, the problem and the remedy you are requesting. Include a product photo if it helps explain a defect. Do not send passwords, mobile-money PINs, identity documents or unrelated bank statements. Keep your receipt and our reply.

Unavailable or undelivered products

If we cannot supply an unavailable item, we will notify you and refund payments as required by law. We will not replace the item or convert your payment into store credit without your agreement. If an agreed delivery period is missed, you may exercise the cancellation rights available under section 67 of the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act, including seven days' written notice where applicable.

Cooling-off rights

Where section 66 of that Act applies, goods may be cancelled within seven days after receipt without a penalty; only the direct return cost may be charged. A refund under that section is due within thirty days of cancellation. Statutory exceptions exist, including certain personalised, perishable or unsealed digital goods. Those exceptions do not remove separate rights concerning defective or misdescribed goods.

Faulty, damaged or incorrect goods

Contact us with the order details and a description of the problem. The remedy and return arrangements must respect your statutory rights; we do not apply a blanket “no refunds” rule. Contact us before shipping a return, because no walk-in return address is currently available.

Deposits and timing

A deposit is part of your payment, not an automatic forfeiture. Statutory rights also apply to deposits. We will explain the refund amount, method and applicable deadline in writing. Additional voluntary return periods and operating arrangements have not yet been finalised; legal deadlines still apply.

If a complaint is unresolved

You may contact Zambia's Competition and Consumer Protection Commission. See the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act, 2021 for the full rules and exceptions.
