How to read these figures

Visits count recorded product-page views, including repeat views, not unique people or all website visits. Optional analytics depends on visitor permission. Financial figures describe orders created during the selected period and their current payment flags; they are not a bank reconciliation or payment-date cash-flow report. Cancelled orders are excluded from order value and unpaid balances. Product counts are current.
